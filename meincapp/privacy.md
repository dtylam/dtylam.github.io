## 2. Privacy Policy

Your privacy and financial confidentiality are fundamental principles of Me Inc. We believe your personal balance sheet and financial affairs are strictly your own business.

### Local-First Data Storage
- **On-Device Database:** All of your balance sheets, accounts, assets, liabilities, transactions, categories, goals, and portfolio records are stored directly on your personal device in a local database.
- **No Remote Servers:** We do not operate external application servers or databases that store, copy, analyze, or process your financial entries.

### Private Apple iCloud Sync
- **CloudKit Integration:** If you choose to enable multi-device sync, data is synchronized exclusively through your personal Apple iCloud account using Apple's native CloudKit technology (Container Identifier: `iCloud.com.dojobills.meincapp`).
- **Zero Developer Access:** Synchronization occurs directly between your devices and Apple's secure iCloud servers using Apple's encryption. The developer has zero access to your iCloud account, private encryption keys, or stored financial figures.

### Zero Third-Party Trackers & Advertising
- **No Advertising SDKs:** Me Inc contains no advertising networks, tracking scripts, or ad brokers.
- **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not embed third-party analytics libraries (such as Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Firebase Analytics, or Mixpanel).
- **No Data Monetization:** We do not sell, rent, license, trade, or share any personal information, financial data, or usage metrics with data brokers or third parties.

## 3. UK GDPR & Data Protection Act 2018 Disclosures

For users located in the United Kingdom (UK) and the European Economic Area (EEA), this section explains how your information is handled under the **UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)** and the **Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA 2018)**.

### Data Controller
The Data Controller responsible for personal data processed in connection with Me Inc is:  
**Donald Lam**  
Contact: <a href="mailto:meincapp@outlook.com">meincapp@outlook.com</a>

### Lawful Basis for Processing
Because Me Inc operates locally on your device and through your private iCloud account, the developer does not collect or process your personal financial data. Any local data processing performed by the app on your device is carried out under the lawful basis of **contractual necessity** (to provide the features and functionality of the app you requested). When you contact us via email, your email address and correspondence are processed under our **legitimate interests** (to respond to and resolve your inquiry).

### Your Data Subject Rights
Under UK GDPR and DPA 2018, you possess rights concerning personal data:

1. **Right of Access & Data Portability:** You have full ownership of your data. You can access and export a complete, unencrypted JSON backup file of your entire financial dataset at any time via **Settings &rarr; Data Management &rarr; Export Backup**.
2. **Right to Rectification:** You can modify, correct, or update any entry, account, transaction, or setting at any time directly within the application.
3. **Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"):** You can delete individual balance sheets, categories, or transactions at any time. You can also permanently delete all application data by removing the app from your device and deleting its iCloud container data from your Apple ID iCloud Storage settings.
4. **Right to Restrict or Object to Processing:** Because all data remains local and under your control, you can cease processing by deleting the data or uninstalling the app.
5. **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** If you believe your privacy rights have been infringed, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK supervisory authority:  
   **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**  
   Website: [https://ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)  
   Helpline: 0303 123 1113

Me Inc provides optional premium features through **Me Inc Pro**:

### Subscription Tiers & Lifetime Access
- **Me Inc Pro Lifetime:** A one-time, non-consumable purchase providing perpetual access with no recurring charges.
- **Me Inc Pro Annual:** An auto-renewable subscription billed annually. Includes an introductory **6-month free trial** for eligible new subscribers.
- **Me Inc Pro Monthly:** An auto-renewable subscription billed monthly. Includes an introductory **6-month free trial** for eligible new subscribers.

### Billing, Renewal & Cancellation
- Payment will be charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase, or after the 6-month free trial concludes.
- Subscriptions automatically renew unless auto-renew is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current trial or billing period.
- Your Apple ID account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period at the then-current subscription price.
- You can manage, modify, or cancel your subscriptions at any time via your **Apple ID Account Settings** on iOS (`Settings -> Apple ID -> Subscriptions`).

### Refunds & Statutory 14-Day Cooling-Off Rights
- **Managed Exclusively by Apple:** All billing, payment transactions, renewals, and refunds are managed directly by Apple via your Apple ID account.
- **UK / EU Consumer Rights:** For consumers in the United Kingdom and European Union, statutory 14-day cancellation and refund rights are administered through Apple's billing platform.
- **Requesting a Refund:** To request a refund or resolve a billing issue, please visit Apple's official portal:
